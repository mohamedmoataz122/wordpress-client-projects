# Daoud Group

Live: https://daoudgroup.com/

Corporate website for Daoud Group, an Egyptian industrial group with companies in manufacturing, trade, construction, utilities, investment and education.

## My role

WordPress developer. I built the theme from the provided designs and set up the admin side so the client can manage content on their own.

## What I built

- Home page with a hero slider, a highlights gallery and the divisions list
- Divisions and Companies as custom post types. Each company belongs to a division and has its own logo, link and contact email. The client can choose which ones show on the home page and in the logo carousel.
- About Us page (mission, vision, milestones, numbers, values, core competencies), all editable from CMB2 fields
- Careers page with an application form. Applicants upload their CV as a PDF and it's emailed to HR as an attachment.
- Contact form sent over AJAX, with email delivered through Mailgun
- News section using normal WordPress posts
- Theme Options page for contact details, social links and footer content

## Stack

WordPress, PHP, custom theme, CMB2, custom post types, AJAX, JavaScript
